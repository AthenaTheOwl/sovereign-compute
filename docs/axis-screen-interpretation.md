# The minimum axis is a conditional capacity screen

Method update: October 18, 2026. Proposed experiments below are not reported as executed results.

`scorecard.py` takes the minimum of four per-axis GW proxies and labels close minima as multiple binding constraints. Its per-axis bands are mechanically set around the supplied values, rather than estimated from observed prediction errors. The resulting ranking is a transparent fixture calculation; the bands are not a calibrated confidence statement about a country's delivery.

The next proposed sensitivity table increases one axis at a time while leaving the others fixed. In a synthetic program with power at 2 GW and silicon at 1 GW, improving power alone cannot lift the minimum above 1 GW. Relaxing silicon may simply reveal water or talent as the next constraint. Report that sequence rather than treating constraints as independent additive shortfalls.

Cross-program aggregation also needs a shared-resource check. Two programs may assume access to the same unallocated accelerator or equipment pool; summing their standalone minima can overstate simultaneous feasibility. Preserve those competition assumptions in scenarios, with dated public sources for factual inputs. No country ranking here establishes a geopolitical prediction or a recommended investment.
